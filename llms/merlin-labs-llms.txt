## What Merlin is

- Founded 2018. Headquarters: 100 Causeway Street, Boston, MA 02114, USA.
- Flight test facilities: Kerikeri (New Zealand), Quonset Point (Rhode Island),
  Bedford / Hanscom Field (Massachusetts).
- Publicly traded on Nasdaq as MRLN since March 2026, via a business combination with
  Inflection Point Acquisition Corp IV.
- Product: the Merlin Pilot — machine-learning mission planning running in parallel with
  deterministic, hardcoded fallback flight systems.
- Programs: prime contractor to USSOCOM for C-130J autonomy; USAF KC-135 tanker autonomy.
- Collaborators: Northrop Grumman, Honeywell, GE Aerospace, Israel Aerospace Industries.
- Certification: New Zealand Part 146 aircraft design organization; Stage of Involvement 3
  (SOI 3) with the Civil Aviation Authority of New Zealand, August 2026.
